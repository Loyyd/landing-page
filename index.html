"Management is still investing in the company's growth. Types of capital expenditure: the two most typical forms of business investment are one, expenditures to maintain current operational levels, and two, expenditures to allow for future growth. Capital expenditure can be physical, such as machine or abstract, such as patent. Both intangible and real capital investments are generally regarded as assets since they may be sold when needed. It's crucial to remember that expenditures on maintenance or growth are treated differently from day-to-day operating costs."

Lecture: Capital Expenditure

  • Main Idea: Capital expenditure is investment aimed at maintaining operations or supporting company growth.
  • Two Types: Businesses spend either to keep current activity running or to expand in the future.
  • Examples: CAPEX can be physical, like machinery, or intangible, like patents.
  • Why It Matters: These items are treated as assets, not ordinary day-to-day operating expenses.
